cad your ai builds real machines in

Polykrom is parametric CAD for people and their AI agents. Design parts and assemblies, prove they work by measuring them, simulate them, and get them made. Every step an agent takes is a command you can see and undo, and every claim it makes is checked against the geometry.

watch an agent build

Polykrom is an MCP server. Point Claude Code, or any agent, at it and ask for a part or a machine. With the app open you follow along: every command the agent runs and what it came to, overlaps and gaps marked on the model as it inspects, and its cursor where it works. It's the same app you use, with the same commands.

from an idea to a made part, and back

An agent should be able to take an idea all the way to a part someone can make and test, then learn from what happened. Here is what that loop can do today, and what is next.

ideaTalk to the built-in assistant (Claude, Gemini, OpenAI or a local model) or bring your own agent over MCPtoday
designParametric parts, sketches with constraints, assemblies with joints, standard parts from the librarytoday
verifyExact measures, clearance and interference, joint sweeps to the true contact stopstoday
renderStudio renders of the design with its materialstoday
animateMotion studies to GIF, and robot export to URDFtoday
simulateStatic stress on the part; modal, thermal and dynamics are nextgrowing
makeSTEP, 3MF, sheet-metal flat patterns, a slicer hand-off; printability checks and CAM are nextgrowing
measureAs-built measurements and test results back into the design, for the next revisionplanned

real parts, from their standards

The library's parts are generated from published dimensions, so they are exact solids with named faces that agents can mate to, not meshes. Fasteners and bearings, T-slot extrusions, NEMA steppers with their ratings, GT2 pulleys with their teeth, MGN rails and carriages, T8 lead screws.

your agent, your tools

Any MCP client can drive Polykrom: headless for scripts and evaluations, or the open app so you can watch. The agent gets the same commands as the menus, and the answers come back as measurements.

$ claude mcp add polykrom -- polykrom-cli mcp

drawn to be read at a glance

Polykrom has its own icon language, Facet: each tool is a line drawing with one lime facet, the face the command makes.
