cad for parts that have to work

Polykrom is open-source parametric CAD in pure Rust. Model a part, check the stress on it before you make it, and work with an AI companion that uses the same commands you do.

Raw CAD in, a working robot out

An AI agent took the SO-101's published CAD, LeRobot's open-source arm, and turned it into a robot using Polykrom's tools: 97 parts organised into links, joints found from the servo geometry, a pick-and-place study, and a stress check on the printed arm. Its kinematics land within 0.81 mm of LeRobot's own URDF. The page shows everything it did, and where this version falls short.

{{ICON:extrude}}

Model

  • Sketches with a constraint solver and dimensions driven by parameters
  • Extrude, revolve, sweep, loft, fillet, shell, holes, threads, patterns
  • Assemblies with joints and motion; sheet metal and plastic parts
  • STEP, IGES, 3MF, STL, OBJ, glTF and URDF
{{ICON:analysis_stress}}

Check

Static stress on the part: pick the held faces and the loaded ones, and read the peak stress, the deflection and the safety factor against the material's yield.

Checked against beam theory: a 200 mm cantilever bends within 2% of P L³ / 3 E I.

{{ICON:agent_chat}}

Design with a companion

An AI agent drives Polykrom through the same commands as the menus, over the Model Context Protocol, and checks what it built by measuring it.

Asked for a steel arm that carries 500 N at a safety factor of two, an agent designed a hollow tube that passed an independent check: SF 2.17, 0.165 mm, 198 g.

Your agent, your tools

Polykrom is an MCP server. Point Claude Code, or any MCP client, at it and ask for a part. With the app open it drives the app, so you can follow along: an Agent panel lists every command and what it found, and its checks are drawn on the model. With no app running it works headless.

claude mcp add polykrom -- polykrom-cli mcp

> Make an aluminium L-bracket, 80 × 40 × 50 mm,
  5 mm thick, with two Ø8 holes, and tell me its mass.

Drawn to be read at a glance

Polykrom has its own icon language, Facet: each tool is a line drawing with one coloured facet, the face the command makes. The colour says what kind of work it is.

{{STRIP}}
Polykrom is a fork of SolidCraft by the SolidCraft contributors, and keeps its history. Fixes that help both go upstream. Stress analysis runs Nightfall's verified solver as a separate program.
